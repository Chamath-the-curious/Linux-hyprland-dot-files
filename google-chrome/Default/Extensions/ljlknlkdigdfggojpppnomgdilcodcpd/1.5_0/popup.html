YouTube Play Next Button

Makes YouTube's hidden "Next" button visible on single videos.

Features:

  • 👁️ Shows YouTube's native "Next" button when hidden
  • 🎨 Uses YouTube's original design and functionality
  • 🔄 Works with playlists and regular videos
  • ⌨️ YouTube's native Shift + N shortcut works on all videos
